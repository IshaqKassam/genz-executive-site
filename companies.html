Why this exists

A note from Ishaq.

I have had the best of teachers, mentors and elders, and I want to pass that on. This platform shares what I have learned and gives the next generation guidance and connections to match.

Companies that join in help that happen. Your young people get a place to learn, network and grow, and your business gets leaders who are ready.

  • A structured path for graduates and early-career talent
  • A growing community of emerging leaders beyond your own walls
  • Practical habits of ownership, judgement and trust
  • Led by a serving executive
Two ways companies use it

Learning and development, and readiness for what comes next.

01

Learning and development

Give your graduates and early-career people a structured way to grow. Gravitas is self-paced, forty sessions of about twenty minutes, so it fits around real work. Add talks, workshops and the community so learning turns into habits.

02

Business continuity and succession

Roles open, people move on and bigger seats need ready people. Take your GenZ talent through the trainings and resources now, so they are prepared to step up with clear habits of ownership, judgement and trust.

What a partnership can include

Shaped around your goals.

  • Group access to the Gravitas Program for 20 or more people, at a custom price
  • Talks, workshops and masterclasses for your young talent and your managers
  • Early access to the community and tools for your people
  • A plan we build with you, starting by listening to what you need

Gravitas is not open yet. Companies can join the founding conversation now and help shape it.

Do you promise promotions or results?

No. We never promise a promotion, a raise or a job. We build habits and readiness, and each person owns their own progress.

Will we see our people’s private work?

No. Journals, trackers and personal cards stay private to each learner unless they choose to share them.

Is it a cohort?

Gravitas is individual and self-paced by design. Your people each go at their own pace, and workshops bring them together as a group.

What does it cost?

Custom, based on group size and what you want. Tell us your goal and we reply with options.
