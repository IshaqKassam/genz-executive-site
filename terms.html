Who we are

The GenZ Executive is operated by Ishaq Kassam, trading as The GenZ Executive. Contact: our email.

What you are buying

Our products are digital files: workbooks, templates, planners and journals. Nothing is shipped. Prices are shown in Kenya shillings (KES) and are one-off payments. Founding prices apply until the end date shown on the shop and the home page. After that date, the regular prices shown beside them apply.

Delivery

We email your files to the address you used at checkout within one working day of payment. If you have not received them, check your spam folder and then write to us.

Refunds

If the files do not arrive, will not open, or are not what the product page described, tell us within 7 days of purchase and we will fix it or refund you. Because digital files cannot be returned, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind once the files have been delivered.

How you may use the files

They are for your personal use. Please do not share, resell or publish them. Teams and universities that want to use them with a group can ask us about group access.

Changes
