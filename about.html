About

Built, not born. Let me show you how.

Ishaq Kassam

I was taught well. Now I want to pass it on.

I am Chief Commercial Officer at FinSense Africa. I have had the best of teachers, mentors and elders, along with exposure and life lessons that shaped me. People I meet keep telling me I have so much to share. This platform is how I share it with the world, and how I keep learning too.

I want to bring more guidance and more connections to the next generation, and give them a place to learn, network, upskill and grow. How trust is earned, how decisions get made and how to be heard are skills, and skills can be written down and practised. I am writing the guide I wish everyone starting out had on day one.

I also want companies to be part of it. Teams with learning and development programmes can tap into this network and platform to bring out the best in their next leaders, and businesses worried about continuity can prepare their young talent to step into bigger roles. See how companies can work with us.

What I believe

Three beliefs behind everything here.

01

Guidance changes careers.

The right advice at the right moment is worth years of trial and error. It can be written down, taught and practised.

02

Executives are built.

Leaders are made by habits repeated on ordinary days, not by a title. That is true in a company, a start-up or a one-person business.

03

Lift as you climb.

Everyone who gets help should pass it on to one more person.
