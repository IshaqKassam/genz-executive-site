Why this matters

The gap is access, not ability.

Young people with a well-connected relative learn the unwritten rules early: how to talk to a manager, how to be noticed, how to plan ten years ahead. Everyone else finds out by trial and error, and loses years doing it.

The GenZ Executive closes that gap. It is a practical playbook, free tools, a community and a six-week program, all built from Ishaq’s experience leading teams as a chief commercial officer. Sponsors make sure the people who need it most can use it for free.

  • Built for young professionals in Kenya and across Africa
  • Practical moves to try at work, not theory
  • Positive about this generation. No “lost generation” talk
  • Free core tools stay free for everyone

Our promises to sponsors

Clear, fair and accountable.

Trust is the whole point of this work, so we hold ourselves to a few simple rules.

  • Independence. Sponsors never edit our content, change our advice or buy a favourable verdict on any book, tool or product.
  • Fair selection. Seats go to learners by open, published criteria, not by who knows whom.
  • Learner privacy. We never share a learner’s name or journal with a sponsor. Reports use anonymised results only.
  • Honest reporting. Sponsors get a plain impact note: who joined, how many finished and what changed. Including what did not work.
  • You choose recognition. Named on this page, named quietly in the note, or fully anonymous.
Questions

Before you decide.

Is this a charity or a tax-deductible donation?

No. The GenZ Executive is a social-impact venture, not a registered charity, so sponsorship is a commercial arrangement and is not tax-deductible. Terms are agreed in writing before any money moves.

Where does my money go?

Into the four areas above, in the proportions we agree with you up front. Sponsor funds for seats are used for seats. We report on it.

Does it support Ishaq personally?

It supports the work. A part of any sponsorship covers the time and costs of building and running the programs, which is how Ishaq can keep the free tools free. The split is shown in your agreement.

How do payments work?

In this launch phase we arrange sponsorship directly, by invoice or bank or mobile-money transfer, after a short conversation. There is no online checkout yet.

Can I sponsor a specific person or group?

You can sponsor a cohort or a theme, such as women in finance or a particular campus. We select the individuals openly, so we do not match a sponsor to a named person.

Can my company use this in its own graduate program?

Yes. Employers can also license Six Rungs and the workbooks for their graduates. Ask us when you enquire.
