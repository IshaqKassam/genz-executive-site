What the name means

Executive means one who follows through.

ex-out, through to the end
+
sequiLatin: to follow
→
exsequito follow a thing through to the end
→
executivethe one who carries it out

Long before it was a job title, the word described a way of working: seeing things through. A title says where you sit. Execution says what you do. That is the idea behind everything here, and it is why anyone can start before the title arrives.

01

Built, not born

Vimeundwa, Swahili for “they are built”. Every skill an executive has can be learned, and you can start on Monday.

02

Follow-through over titles

Trust comes from finishing what you start and being clear about it. The seat follows the work.

03

Lift as you climb

A GenZ Executive helps the next person up. The goal is a generation that rises together.

Etymology: from Latin exsequi, “to follow up, carry out” (ex- “out” + sequi “to follow”), via execute. See Wiktionary and Dictionary.com.
