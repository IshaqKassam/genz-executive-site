Inside the book

Three skills that decide how far you go.

01

Be relied on

The small, visible habits that make managers and colleagues trust you with more, starting in your first 90 days.

02

Be heard

Say the answer first, write one clear page and ask for what you need with confidence.

03

Build on purpose

Judgement, money habits, learning and a network you grow deliberately.

Guidance can be written down, taught and practised.

Is this for you?

Written for every rung of the climb.

  • Graduates and job seekers about to enter the workplace
  • Young professionals in their first roles who want to stand out
  • New managers leading people for the first time
  • Mentors and employers who want to guide a generation well
When is it out?

2027. Format and price will be confirmed first to the launch list.

Does it work in my industry?

Yes. The habits apply anywhere trust and judgement matter, from a hospital to a start-up to a family business.

Can I start before the book is out?

Yes. The workbooks in the shop are built to use today, and Six Rungs is a six-week program on the same ideas.
