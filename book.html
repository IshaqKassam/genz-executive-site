Inside the book

Three ideas the book is built on.

01

Earn trust fast

The small, visible moves that make managers and colleagues rely on you, in your first 90 days and after.

02

Communicate like an executive

Say the answer first, write one clear page, and ask for what you need without apology.

03

Build, not just wait

Judgement, money, learning and a network you grow on purpose. Built, not born.

Not a lost generation. An unguided one.

Who it is for

Written for the first ten years of your career.

  • Graduates and job seekers about to enter the workplace
  • Young professionals in their first roles, wondering how to stand out
  • New managers leading people for the first time
  • Mentors and employers who want to guide a generation well
When is it out?

The launch date, format and price are still to be announced. Join the launch list and you will hear first.

Is it only for people in banking or tech?

No. The examples come from African banking and tech, but the habits apply in any workplace where trust and judgement matter.

Can I start before the book is out?

Yes. The workbooks and templates in the shop are built to be used this week, and The Climb is a six-week program on the same ideas.
