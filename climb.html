The six rungs

One rung a week. Top by week six.

1Week 1

Map and aim

Know where you stand, what your organisation rewards and what you are aiming for in your first 90 days.

2Week 2

Deliver a quick win

Choose one visible, useful result you can finish early, and make it count.

3Week 3

Communicate like an executive

Lead with the answer, write one clear page and ask for what you need.

4Week 4

Read the room

Understand who matters, how decisions get made and what people need from you.

5Week 5

Show up

Build the reputation that comes from reliability, follow-through and presence.

6Week 6

Lead it

Take ownership of something end to end and bring other people with you.

A day in The Climb

Twenty minutes. Learn, try, note.

1

Learn

A short read on the business thinking behind the day’s habit.

2

Try

One small move you make at work today. No permission needed.

3

Note

Two lines in your private journal about what happened and what you noticed.

What you get

Everything you need to keep going.

  • Thirty daily moves across six weeks
  • A private journal, habit tracker and 90-day planner
  • Nothing shared with your manager or anyone else
  • A founding cohort of young professionals doing it with you
