The idea

A generation told it is lost. It is not. It is unguided.

Nobody hands you the unwritten rules of work: how trust is built, how decisions get made, how to be heard. The GenZ Executive writes them down, so you can start acting like an executive from your first week.

What you will learn

Three skills that move careers.

You can practise all three from week one. None of them needs a title.

01

Earn trust fast

The small, visible moves that make managers and colleagues rely on you, in your first 90 days and after.

02

The book

What the first ten years at work should have taught you.

An executive is someone who owns outcomes, uses judgement and earns trust. The book shows how, with African examples and a plan you can start on Monday.

  • Practical, not theory: every idea ends in something you do
  • Written for Africa’s banks, startups and growing firms
  • A 90-day plan you can start straight away

The Climb

A six-week mini MBA in earning trust.

Thirty short daily moves, about twenty minutes each. You learn the business thinking behind each habit, try it at work, and keep a private journal. Nothing is shared with your manager or anyone else.
