The book

What the first ten years at work should have taught you.

An executive is someone who owns outcomes, uses judgement and earns trust. You can do all three from your first week. The book shows how, with African examples and a plan you can start on Monday.

01

Earn trust fast

The small, visible moves that make managers and colleagues rely on you, in your first 90 days and after.

02

Communicate like an executive

Say the answer first, write one clear page, and ask for what you need without apology.

03

Build, not just wait

Judgement, money, learning and a network you grow on purpose. Built, not born.

The Climb

A six-week mini MBA in earning trust.

Thirty short daily moves, about twenty minutes each. You learn the business thinking behind each habit, try it at work, and keep a private journal, habit tracker and 90-day planner. Nothing is shared with your manager or anyone else.
