Four quick wins

Four moves that change how you are seen.

01

Get noticed

The move: send a five-line Friday update to your manager. Visible work is trusted work.

“Done this week, next week, one thing I need.”
02

Get heard

The move: answer first, reasons second. One sentence that makes a room listen.

“My recommendation is X. Here is why.”
03

Get clear

The move: ask one question in week one that turns guesswork into a clear brief.

“What does a great first 90 days look like to you?”
04

Get direction

The move: picture yourself in ten years, work back to this quarter, and check in every Friday.

“What is the one thing this quarter that moves me forward?”
