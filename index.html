You are not behind. You were never briefed.

Nobody writes down how trust gets built, who actually decides, or what a good one-page update looks like. Most people learn it by getting it wrong, slowly. This puts it on paper so you can skip the slow part.

Sound familiar?

Four things nobody explains, and the move that fixes each.

01

“I work hard and nobody notices.”

The move: a five-line Friday update to your manager. Visible work is trusted work.

02

“I go quiet in meetings.”

The move: answer first, reasons second. One sentence that makes people listen.

03

“I do not know what my manager really wants.”

The move: one question in week one that turns guessing into a clear brief.

04

“I want to grow but there is no map.”

The move: a ten-year picture worked back to this quarter, checked every Friday.
