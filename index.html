The Climb

Six weeks. Twenty minutes a day. One habit per day.

Each day: read the thinking, make one small move at work, write two lines in your journal. By week six you have thirty moves behind you and a record of what worked. Everything stays private.

Why I wrote it
The talent was there. What was missing was guidance.

I am the Chief Commercial Officer at FinSense Africa, a software consultancy that serves banks. I lead sales, marketing and operations, and I coach the people in those teams.

Working with young professionals, I kept seeing the same gap: how to be heard in a meeting, how to make your work visible, how to be relied on. Nobody had explained it. So I started the #GenZExecutive movement. This generation is not lost. It is unguided, and guidance can be written down and practised.
