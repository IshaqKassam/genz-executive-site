The six marks of gravitas

One mark a week. Five moves each.

From curiosity to clarity, one mark at a time. Each week builds one visible mark of someone people rely on. Four learning moves, then a Friday check. Every move ends with something you can point to.

1
Week 1 · the mark of

Clarity — Map and aim

Know where you stand, what your organisation really rewards and what you are aiming for in your first 90 days.

By Friday you haveA one-page map of your role, how your company makes money, and a 90-day aim with one measure.
  1. Name your starting point and take your baseline
  2. Learn what your organisation really rewards
  3. Draw how the company makes money, on one page
  4. Set a 90-day aim with one measurable result
  5. Friday check. Say your aim in two sentences.

Backed by: The First 90 Days; What the CEO Wants You to Know

2
Week 2 · the mark of

Delivery — Deliver a quick win

Choose one visible, useful result you can finish inside a week, and make it count.

By Friday you haveOne finished win, with proof logged and feedback asked for.
  1. Pick one quick win: visible, useful, finishable
  2. Agree what “done” means in one line
  3. Do the work and show early progress in three lines
  4. Finish, hand over and close the loop
  5. Friday check. Log your proof line and note who noticed.

Backed by: Atomic Habits; Essentialism; Getting Things Done

3
Week 3 · the mark of

Voice — Communicate like an executive

Lead with the answer, write one clear page and say one useful thing in a meeting.

By Friday you haveFour five-line updates sent, a one-page memo written, one point made in a meeting.
  1. Answer first: rewrite three of your own messages
  2. The Friday five-line update: Done, Next, Need
  3. The one-page memo: problem, options, recommendation, ask
  4. Speak in the meeting: one point in 30 seconds
  5. Friday check. Send your update and review how it landed.

Backed by: How to Win Friends and Influence People; Made to Stick; Radical Candor

4
Week 4 · the mark of

Judgement — Read the room

Understand who matters, how decisions really get made and what each person needs from you.

By Friday you haveA stakeholder map, a plan for one difficult behaviour, one hard conversation held.
  1. Map your stakeholders by power and interest
  2. Know the cast: respond to behaviour, not labels
  3. Hold one hard conversation well
  4. Read one number your team owns
  5. Friday check. Review your map and what changed.

Backed by: Emotional Intelligence; Crucial Conversations

5
Week 5 · the mark of

Presence — Show up

Build the reputation that comes from reliability, calm under pressure and visible follow-through.

By Friday you haveA promise ledger with commitments closed, a meeting etiquette checklist applied, a calm routine for pressure.
  1. Learn the unwritten rules of meetings
  2. Keep a promise ledger and close every commitment
  3. Practise stillness before reaction
  4. Be visible without showing off
  5. Friday check. Count the promises you closed.

Backed by: Stillness Is the Key; Deep Work; The Effective Executive

6
Week 6 · the mark of

Ownership — Lead it

Take ownership of something end to end and bring other people with you.

By Friday you haveA one-page business case pitched, one person helped, and your next 90 days planned.
  1. Spot a problem you can own
  2. Build the case on one page: cost, benefit, risk, time
  3. Pitch it and handle “no” or “not now”
  4. Lift one person and plan your next 90 days
  5. Friday check. Take your end baseline and assemble your portfolio.

Backed by: Extreme Ownership; Good to Great

The week and the day

Twenty minutes. Five small parts.

MonOrient and move 1
TueMove 2
WedMove 3
ThuMove 4
FriCheck and proof line
Sat–SunRest or catch up
Know

A short lesson with a weak and a strong example, and a template.

Try

One small move you make at work today. No permission needed.

Say

Optional: rehearse out loud before the moment.

Notice

What happened, what you felt, what you would change.

Log

One proof line. Your portfolio builds itself.

You take a short baseline before move one and again after move thirty, so you can see your own change across the six marks. Pause once if life gets in the way and finish within eight weeks.

What you leave with

Your Gravitas Portfolio.

Not a certificate for the wall. Proof you can show a manager, use in a promotion conversation or bring to an interview.

  • Your 90-day aim, on one page
  • Your quick win, with proof and feedback
  • Four five-line updates and one one-page memo
  • Your stakeholder map and a plan for one hard relationship
  • Your promise ledger
  • Your one-page business case
  • Your start and end baseline across the six marks
  • Your next 90 days

A certificate of completion comes with it. It is not an accredited qualification, and we will never promise a promotion, a raise or a job.

Built for where you are

Three tracks. Same six marks.

Most young Africans are not in a corporate office, and the program does not pretend they are. Pick the track that fits your life.

At work

You are employed, in any role. Every move is written for your desk, your manager and your meetings.

Intern or first month

Use your attachment, project team or mentor as the workplace. Smaller wins, plus a move on asking for a clear brief.

Student or job seeker

Use a campus club, a volunteer project, a side hustle or a family business as your workplace. Your portfolio doubles as interview evidence.

Ways to take it

Pick the format that fits.

Self-paced

The full program on your own schedule: the thirty moves, tools, library, portfolio and certificate of completion. Questions? Ask Ishaq one to one.

Cohort

Everything in self-paced, with a shared start date, an accountability partner and a community thread to keep you moving.

Teams

For employers and campuses with a group of learners. A facilitator guide, and only anonymised totals are ever shared, never individual journals.

Sponsored places

A free place for someone who cannot pay, made possible by gifts to the initiative.

Pricing is shared with the waitlist first. Everything is private by default: no manager or employer ever sees your journal.

What we promise

Honest outcomes.

  • A clear 90-day aim and a documented win
  • The answer-first habit, with updates and a memo you have actually sent
  • A plan for one difficult relationship at work
  • A portfolio you can show

We measure completion, your own before-and-after ratings and what you tell us at 30 and 90 days, and we will share the results honestly, including where we fall short.

Will my employer see anything?

No. Your journal and tracker are private to you.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It is a certificate of completion backed by your portfolio of work.

Can I get started today?

Yes. The 90-Day Trust Build workbook uses the same ideas and works on your own schedule.
