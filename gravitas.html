The six marks of gravitas

One mark a week. Five days each.

From curiosity to clarity, one mark at a time. Each week builds one visible quality of someone people rely on, and ends with something you can point to.

The rhythm

Twenty minutes. Five days a week.

MonOpen the week
TueMove 2
WedMove 3
ThuMove 4
FriReview and evidence
Sat–SunRest or catch up

Each day is a short, practical rhythm: one idea, one action and one line of evidence. The details are shared with learners.

What you leave with

Your Gravitas Portfolio.

Not a certificate for the wall. Proof you can show a manager, use in a promotion conversation or bring to an interview.

  • Your 90-day target, on one page
  • Finished work you can point to
  • Evidence that people noticed
  • A plan for your next 90 days

A certificate of completion comes with it. It is not an accredited qualification, and we will never promise a promotion, a raise or a job.

Built for where you are

Three tracks. Same six marks.

Most young Africans are not in a corporate office, and the program does not pretend they are. Pick the track that fits your life.

At work

You are employed, in any role. Every move is written for your desk, your manager and your meetings.

Intern or first month

Use your attachment, project team or mentor as the workplace. Smaller wins, plus a move on asking for a clear brief.

Student or job seeker

Use a campus club, a volunteer project, a side hustle or a family business as your workplace. Your portfolio doubles as interview evidence.

When does it start?

How much will it cost?

How much time does it take?

About twenty minutes a day, Monday to Friday, for six weeks.

Do I need a corporate job?

No. There are tracks for interns, students and job seekers, using a club, a project or a side hustle as your workplace.

Can I ask Ishaq a question?

Yes. Learners get one-to-one access to Ishaq for questions about the program and their situation.

Will my employer see anything?

No. Your journal and tracker are private to you.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It is a certificate of completion backed by your portfolio of work.

Can I get started today?

Yes. The 90-Day Trust Build workbook uses the same ideas and works on your own schedule.
