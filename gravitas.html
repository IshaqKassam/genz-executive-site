The ideas behind every session

Four ideas you carry long after day forty.

01

The bamboo tree

The Chinese bamboo tree spends its first years growing roots, then shoots up in weeks. Growth you cannot see yet is still growth. Gravitas is built on patience and roots first.

02

Trade-offs and the long view

Every choice gives something up. You learn to weigh what you gain and give up, and to ask how a decision looks in five years, not just this week.

03

Social capital in big decisions

The trust and goodwill you build travel with you. You learn to weigh what a career move does to your relationships and reputation, and to build that goodwill on purpose.

04

A positive mind

Not pretending everything is fine, but asking what is still possible and what a setback has taught you. It is how steady people keep moving.

Is it for you?

Honest about who it helps.

It is for you if

  • You want to grow into the executive you could be, at any stage: starting out, stepping up, already leading a team, freelancing, running a business or changing direction
  • You are ready to change how you work, not just collect ideas
  • You can give twenty minutes a day for a few weeks
  • You are willing to write things down and try them at work, in a club or in your own project

It is not for you if

  • You want a promotion, a raise or a job promised. We never promise those
  • You want a lecture series to watch. This is short practice, not video
  • You want an accredited qualification. The certificate is of completion
