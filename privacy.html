How we handle your details

Last updated 19 November 2026.

This page explains what The GenZ Executive collects, why, and your choices. The site is run by Ishaq Kassam, trading as The GenZ Executive. Questions go to our email.

What we collect

  • Launch list and community registry: your name, email, what you want to hear about, and your career stage if you tell us. You give this on the sign-up form.
  • Orders: name, email and order details, so we can send your files. Payments run on the payment provider’s own secure checkout, so we never see your card number or M-Pesa PIN.
  • Enquiries: whatever you put in your email to us.

Why we use it

We use launch-list details only to tell you about the book, Gravitas, the community and workshops. We use order details to deliver your files and answer questions about them. We do not sell your details.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are stored in a Google Form and spreadsheet owned by The GenZ Executive. Order details are held by our payment provider and in our email. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. The scorecard runs in your browser, and your answers are not sent anywhere unless you choose to join the list.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to remove you from the list. Every email we send can be unsubscribed from. We keep launch-list details until you ask us to remove them. We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting.

Contact

Write to our email and we will respond promptly.
